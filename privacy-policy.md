# Days Since – Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** November 2025  

Days Since is a simple productivity app developed by microApps.  
We respect your privacy.  

## Data Collection
- The app does **not collect personal data** such as names, emails, or phone numbers.  
- Your counters and dates are stored **locally on your device** and are never transmitted to our servers.  
- The app uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display ads. AdMob may collect:
  - Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  - App usage data and diagnostics for advertising and performance measurement.  
- You can permanently remove ads via a one-time in-app purchase.

## Data Security
- All network communication with AdMob is encrypted via HTTPS.  
- No login or account creation is required.  

## Your Rights
- You can delete all data by uninstalling the app.  
- To manage ad personalization, visit your device’s Google Ads settings.
